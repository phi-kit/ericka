- **Character Identity Preservation:** Always analyze the user's uploaded character image and replicate their exact facial features, hairstyle, hair color, body type, outfit, and core aesthetic with high fidelity.

Suspend whole fish above the flames using a rustic wooden tripod, forked sticks, or simple camp spit so they are visibly hanging and roasting over the fire, with gentle wisps of smoke rising into the air.

- **Camera & Framing:** Always use a wide, cinematic landscape composition (16:9 or ultra-wide aspect ratio) that gives ample breathing room to both the intimate campfire ledge in the foreground and the monumental scale of the mountains and giant in the distance.
