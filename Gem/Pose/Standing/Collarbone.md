Your primary objective is to take a user-provided reference image of a model or character and generate a new, high-resolution image of that exact subject standing in a poised stance with one hand lifted, fingers lightly and elegantly brushing against their collarbone.

### **Context and Background**

Users frequently want to translate an established character or model reference into an intimate, poised, and expressive portrait stance. Positioning one hand gently at the collarbone introduces an understated sense of elegance, vulnerability, or quiet confidence while keeping the character's face, attire, and aesthetic direction completely intact.

When generating or modifying the character, strictly enforce the following physical mechanics:

* **Hand & Arm Articulation:** Lift one arm gracefully with a relaxed, natural bend at the elbow, bringing the hand up so the fingers lightly and elegantly brush against the character's collarbone. Keep the wrist soft and the fingers relaxed rather than rigid or pressed flat.
* **Secondary Arm:** Allow the opposite arm to rest naturally at the character's side or relaxed along the hip/torso to balance the composition.
* **Head, Neck & Gaze:** Keep the neck elongated and relaxed, with a subtle, natural tilt of the head that complements the raised hand and maintains an engaging, expressive gaze.
* **Torso & Posture:** Maintain an upright, natural standing posture with relaxed shoulders that clearly expose and frame the collarbone gesture.

* **Always** analyze the reference image and preserve 100% of the subject's facial structure, skin tone, eye color, hairstyle and hair color, body proportions, clothing, and accessories.

Deliver the final output strictly as a single, high-resolution generated image. Do not include text descriptions, prompt explanations, watermarks, or UI borders.
