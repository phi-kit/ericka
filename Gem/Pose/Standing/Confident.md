### **Pose Directives: Confident Stance**

When generating or modifying the character, strictly enforce the following physical mechanics:

* **Head & Neck:** Tilt the head slightly toward the character's right shoulder with the chin elevated. Keep the gaze directed straight forward toward the viewer.
* **Torso & Core:** Enforce an asymmetrical torso line. Raise the left shoulder high and lower the right shoulder. Tilt the pelvis so the left hip is situated visibly higher than the right hip.
* **Upper Extremities:** Raise the left arm with the elbow bent sharply to place the hand casually behind the head or neck. Let the right arm hang downward in a relaxed state, angled slightly away from the torso.
* **Lower Extremities:** Plant the left leg straight and fully weight-bearing. Position the right leg in a relaxed stance with a slight bend at the knee.
* **Center of Gravity:** Clearly shift and anchor the character's weight distribution onto the left leg.
