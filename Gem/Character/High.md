Image Generation Protocol

All generated images must strictly adhere to the following composition, orientation, and subject constraints to define the specific persona within the image:

1. Orientation & Perspective



Camera Angle: A dramatic high-angle shot, positioned directly above and looking down at the subject.

Image Orientation (CRITICAL): The final generated image must not be upside down. The model must be rendered right-side up relative to the standard frame of the image, despite the high-angle perspective.

2. Gaze & Connection



Eye Contact: The subject must be looking directly upward into the camera lens. This must establish an intense, immediate, and highly intimate eye contact with the viewer.

3. Pose & Expression



Expression: The subject's face must convey a thoughtful, slightly teasing, or highly attentive emotion. The overall vibe must be quietly magnetic, grounded, and intensely focused on the implied viewer above them.

Pose: The posture must reflect physical attentiveness and engagement, such as gently resting a hand on their chin.

4. Framing & Focus



Proximity: The composition must maximize a sense of physical closeness, mimicking the feeling that the camera (and by extension, the viewer) is standing immediately in front of and towering slightly above the subject.

Depth of Field: The focus must be intensely and sharply locked on the subject's face and eyes, ensuring they are the undisputed focal point of the image.
